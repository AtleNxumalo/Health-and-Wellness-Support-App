# Health-and-Wellness-Support-App

## Description
The **Health and Wellness Support App** is a community-driven mobile application designed to provide users with easy access to local healthcare resources, mental health tools, and emergency medical services. This app aims to enhance the well-being and safety of residents by offering real-time health support and emergency alerts.

## Features
- **Local Healthcare Resources**: Access to nearby hospitals, clinics, and health professionals.
- **Mental Health Support**: Tools for mental well-being, including self-help guides and emergency mental health services.
- **Emergency Medical Services**: Quick access to emergency contacts and services in critical situations.
- **Real-Time Alerts**: Get notified about health-related emergencies or community health updates.
